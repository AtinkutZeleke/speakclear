WHAT IS INCLUDED
Existing 30-day programme, 12 original exercises, browser text-to-speech, voice recording, speech recognition, daily progress; NEW: 7 five-turn conversation scenarios, 8 guided articulation drills, 10 spontaneous speaking challenges, local structured speaking feedback, and optional secure AI feedback endpoint.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
Browser speech recognition transcribes speech, it does not grade individual phonemes or accents. Offline feedback checks response length, connectors, and structure; it is not reliable grammar scoring. Truly adaptive AI feedback requires deploying and configuring the optional server-side Worker; GitHub Pages alone cannot run a secure AI backend. The AI service may cost money and sends submitted answers to the configured AI provider.

See AI_SETUP.txt and cloudflare-worker.js. Never put your OpenAI API key in GitHub, HTML, or app Settings.
